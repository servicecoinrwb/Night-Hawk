MODES

Action runThe full drive. Three hunter crews — rammers on your bumper, blade cars alongside, gun cars out front shooting back. Three panels of hull.
Cruise runSame road, nobody hunting. Weapons stowed, five panels, denser traffic. Just you, the road work and the spills.
Night DepositThe bank job. Roof entry, four floors down to the server room, then run the whole route backwards before the bell.
Dark FiberThe side job. Sneak the floor, read what people left lying around, put guards down quietly. One way to blow it: let a guard fill his meter.

NOTES

SoundtrackWritten in code, not recorded — a four-bar minor progression sequenced live in Web Audio. Tempo and filter cutoff track your speed, and the layers stack as hostiles arrive. No audio files, nothing to load.
Built withTwo single HTML files. three.js r128 off a CDN, everything else hand-rolled. No build step, no framework, no dependencies to install — open the file and it runs.
ScoresBest score and longest run are held in memory for the session only. They reset when you reload.
